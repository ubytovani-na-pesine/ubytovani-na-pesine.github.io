Comfortable accommodation within easy reach of the city centre, free parking

Two private ground-floor apartments in a family house located in Dolní Chabry, a charming residential district with excellent access to Prague city centre. These comfortable studio apartments, each with a kitchen and bathroom, are ideal for guests who value comfort, privacy and a quiet atmosphere while still enjoying easy access to the heart of the city.

Free parking is available on the property or directly in front of the house. A public transport stop is just around the corner. The metro station is 8 minutes away by bus, and the city centre is approximately 20 minutes away by public transport.

Several restaurants, a café, a confectionery shop and a Billa supermarket are all within walking distance.
